jobs · we are hiring

Join the most hardcore robotics crew in Switzerland.

We're looking for the people who don't quite fit in, the makers, the builders bursting with creativity and suffering from a burning need to express it. The engineers at heart, the artists, the salesmen, the machinists and the people with so much passion that it infects the rest of the world.

The people who don't see their jobs as a chore but as a means to express their true potential, as a way to grow in the world, to find their purpose, their true calling. We have vested interests in the real world, the one of atoms and electrons. We're working on the hardest problems we've come across and understand that this will be a decade long mission.

We're on a quest to find the people who want to impact this physical world, the engineers who enjoy writing software for the moment they hit deploy, they sit back, lift their heads up and get to watch a pile of metal come to life centimeters from their excited eyes. Bringing back long-forgotten childhood memories of playing with toy robots, imagining that, maybe one day, they would make those toys for real.

Well, that day has come. The place to make that dream a reality is at the bottom of this page.

We don't care where you graduated or how many years you've worked. Just make sure you're aligned with our values. If you're a fit, the rest will follow.

Loïc Delineau CEO, coprod
